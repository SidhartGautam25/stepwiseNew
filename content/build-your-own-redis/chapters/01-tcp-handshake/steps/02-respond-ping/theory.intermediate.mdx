### Theory: Bulk Strings and Framing

In RESP2, commands sent by redis-cli and official drivers are always framed as Arrays (`*<count>\r\n`) of Bulk Strings (`$<length>\r\n<payload>\r\n`).

For example, `PING` is framed as:
```
*1\r\n
$4\r\n
PING\r\n
```

Simple Strings are framed with a leading `+` and CRLF:
```
+PONG\r\n
```
Because Simple Strings cannot contain binary null bytes or CRLF characters, they are ideal for lightweight status confirmations.
